# ClearDesk Support Handbook

Fictional sample document created for this assessment. ClearDesk and every policy below are demonstration data, not PointStar's policies.

[S1] Support channels
Contact support@cleardesk.example for assistance. Support hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 Malaysia time. The target first response is one business day; this is a target rather than a guarantee.

[S2] Plans
The Starter plan costs MYR 20 per user per month. The Team plan costs MYR 35 per user per month. These sample prices exclude taxes. Monthly fees are the plan price multiplied by the number of users.

[S3] Fictional refund policy
A first subscription payment may be refunded if the customer requests it within 14 calendar days of that payment and has used fewer than 2 hours of service. Renewal payments are non-refundable under this sample policy. Support must verify the payment date and usage before determining eligibility; the assistant cannot approve or issue a refund.

[S4] Technical escalation
Reports of data loss, a service outage, or a security breach require human support before a response is drafted. A customer with more than 3 distinct support contacts in the preceding 7 days also requires human support.

[S5] Account access
For a forgotten password, use the Forgot password link on the sign-in page. Support will never ask customers to send a password or a recovery code. Suspected unauthorized access must be escalated to human support.

[S6] Cancellation
Customers can cancel renewal from Settings, then Billing, then Cancel renewal. Access continues until the end of the paid billing period. Cancellation alone does not imply a refund.
